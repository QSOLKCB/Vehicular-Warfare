# Operation Overlord

Choose **Operation Overlord · Tactical Intelligence** on the mission board. Twelve Cobalt crews and twelve Ember crews start in their Big Battle deployment. Disable the shore relay and two mobile defenders, then occupy the crossing with a surviving crew before the two-minute timer expires. Your crews can continue following their doctrines while you watch from the map or Battle Director.

Select crews on the map or roster. **Assign** changes their autonomous doctrine. Use MANUAL to stop automatic engagements when a crew has no order. An explicit order takes precedence, including queued waypoints. For a section task, select ADVANCE, COVER, FLANK or REGROUP, enter a chart position and choose **Issue task**. This replaces the selected crews’ explicit queues. A nearby active COVER task can hold an ADVANCE group until the supporting crew arrives or the bounded wait expires. The route can still fail on the physical board.

The roster shows doctrine, hull status and the executing controller. **Faction contacts** lists the reporting crew, observation age and observed coordinates. A filled enemy symbol requires a sampled contact and current physical visibility. A dashed ring stays at the reported position after sight is lost. The strategic and 3D displays omit unseen live pieces. The tactical observer omits historical debris because an old impact has no contemporaneous observer receipt.

Open **Decision inspector** with a crew selected. The tick, target, score and observation source come from the crew's most recent accepted decision. **Subject decision** follows the camera subject in Battle Director, including during paused or verified-frame inspection. Camera controls, display preferences and selection never enter the command stream.

From pause or debrief choose **Save replay**. Reopen it on this mission's briefing using **Open replay**. The version 3 receipt for breakwater-overlord-v1 reconstructs the board and faction decisions from the seed and accepted commands. Verification checks the state and combat-event digests in the recording runtime. The existing mission entries continue to verify their own receipts.
